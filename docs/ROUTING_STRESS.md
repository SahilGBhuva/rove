# Routing stress results

Generated by `node scripts/stress-routing.mjs`. 131 real origin/destination pairs (geocoded once with Photon/OpenStreetMap, cached in `scripts/stress-geocodes.json`) × 5 weekday times (Wednesday 2026-10-21 08:15, 12:30, 17:30, 22:30 and Thursday 00:30) = 655 requests to `planExperimental`. All trips come from the bundled GTFS timetables, which contain BART, Muni, Caltrain, VTA and AC Transit only.

Definitions:

- **Success**: at least one practical itinerary. The 3 "no-coverage" pairs (Half Moon Bay, Sausalito, Pacifica: SamTrans, Golden Gate Transit and the ferries are not in the data) are excluded from success rates and must return no route.
- **Duration ratio**: door-to-door time from the requested departure to arrival, divided by the straight-line distance at 20 km/h (floor 10 min). Lower is better.
- **One-seat baseline**: an independent, naive scan for the earliest single-vehicle ride from stops within 800 m of each end (same walking model as the planner). `SLOWER_THAN_ONE_SEAT`: the top option has transfers and arrives more than 2 min later than that ride. `MISSED_ONE_SEAT`: no option is one-seat although the direct ride arrives within 10 min of the best option.
- `WALK_FASTER`: under 1.6 km apart and walking (the planner model: 1.25 × straight line at 1.2 m/s) beats the top option by more than a minute. Walk distances are straight-line estimates, as the planner reports them.

## What the stress run found and what changed

Each fix was re-run on the full suite. Numbers are for the 640 requests inside coverage. Query time is CPU time on a machine whose load average was 60 to 180 during these runs, so treat it as relative.

| Step | Success | Daytime | p50 / p90 ratio | Mean transfers | p90 walk m | SLOWER / MISSED one-seat | LONG_WALK | FAR_ENDPOINT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (e2adcf9) | 83.4% | 97.7% | 1.43 / 2.99 | 1.01 | 1308 | 10 / 9 | 71 | 38 |
| 1. Served-only, route-diverse access stops; next-morning window | 88.1% | 98.4% | 1.38 / 2.96 | 0.81 | 1756 | 1 / 0 | 134 | 79 |
| 2. Walk timing ×1.25 circuity, long-walk-leg penalty, adaptive radius | 86.4% | 98.4% | 1.44 / 3.10 | 0.97 | 1346 | 4 / 1 | 66 | 37 |
| 3. Radius counts only patterns running near the request time; typed-array scans | 87.5% | 98.4% | 1.45 / 3.14 | 0.97 | 1394 | 4 / 1 | 74 | 41 |
| 4. Short-hop filter, direct-walk note, severe-backtrack rule, baseline on the same walking model | 87.3% | 98.4% | 1.45 / 3.14 | 0.94 | 1394 | 2 / 0 | 78 | 40 |
| 5. Walk-instead variants | 87.7% | 98.4% | 1.45 / 3.14 | 0.92 | 1408 | 2 / 0 | 80 | 49 |

From step 2 on, ratios are not directly comparable with the earlier rows: walks are now timed 25% slower (more honestly), so door-to-door durations rise even where the plan is identical.

Failure and bad-recommendation classes found, with root causes:

1. **Candidate stops too few, or not real stops.** The bundled stops include 369 GTFS entrances, elevators and parent stations with no service. They took the "4 BART" slots and the footpath slots. From the Ferry Building the Embarcadero platforms (430 m away) were never seeded, so the planner rode Muni one stop and changed to the Yellow line and then a bus. The 12 nearest stops were also often all on one line: Coit Tower had only the 39, so at 22:30 there was no route at all. Fix: served-only, route-pattern-diverse access stops.
2. **Wrong overnight window.** After noon the planner searched yesterday plus today, so 22:30 requests could never see the next morning. They returned "no connection" with no "first train at" information (23 requests). Fix: today plus tomorrow from 12:00.
3. **Missed one-seat rides.** 9 requests had no one-seat option although a direct ride arrived within 10 minutes of the best option. 10 more had a transfer option arriving more than 2 minutes after the direct ride. Both came from cause 1. Two remain, both near-ties: South San Francisco BART to 24th & Mission, where BART plus the 49 avoids a 700 m walk.
4. **Excessive walking.** Once far stops were reachable, straight-line timing at 1.2 m/s made 1.4 km walks look cheap. Fix: circuity factor, extra cost for any single walk over 8 minutes, and a radius that grows only where service is sparse or at night.
5. **Absurd short hops.** A one-stop bus ride to save a 300 m walk was offered as an alternative. Fix: walk-instead variants plus the short-hop filter.
6. **Absurd backtracking.** Mountain View Transit Center to El Camino Hospital (2.9 km) at 22:30: Caltrain to Sunnyvale and a bus back, 96 minutes. Fix: backtracking beyond max(3 km, trip distance) is not practical. A note gives the walking estimate instead.
7. **Very short trips.** Union Square to Powell Station (290 m) and Diridon to SAP Center (260 m) were offered buses. Fix: `routing.directWalk`, the `WALKING_MAY_BE_FASTER` warning and a plain note. A walking-only itinerary is not invented, because walks are not verified paths.

Remaining failures are data limits (see the worst cases): agencies that are not bundled (Wheels, County Connection, SamTrans, shuttles, ferries) and no elevation. At 22:30 and 00:30 most "failures" are genuine service gaps. They are answered with the first real later trip (`laterItineraries`) when the 8-hour horizon reaches it.
